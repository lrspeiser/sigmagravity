Scientific boundary

A test bench, not a claim of discovery.

This hosted fast path evaluates published radial baryonic mass-model components. It does not pretend that algebraic circular-orbit tests are full AQUAL, QUMOND, or relativistic lensing solutions.

The next worker tier will reuse the repository’s real 2D/3D field solvers and raw image-plane lensing engine. Those runs will be asynchronous, immutable, and scored separately from this preview.
