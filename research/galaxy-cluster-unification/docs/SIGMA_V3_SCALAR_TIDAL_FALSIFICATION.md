## Scope of the falsification

local baryonic tidal tensor and a universal scale-memory have failed the same

| Formulation | Physical question | Median distributed/compact response | Gate |

> A bounded local scalar action density made only from algebraic invariants of

> robust universal compact-versus-distributed discriminator.

It does **not** falsify every nonlinear, nonlocal, tensor, or modified-gravity
theory.  It does not count as a raw galaxy or cluster failure because no
observational holdout was opened.

## Why the class failed

Compact galaxies are not perfectly spherical point sources.  Their disks and
bulges produce changing tidal eigenvalues and eigenvectors across a smoothing
scale, which activates every scalar tested.  Distributed clusters also become
approximately pointlike sufficiently far away.  A volume integral therefore
mixes compact-source internal structure with distributed-source overlap.

High-acceleration screening creates strong separation near
\(g\sim a_\Sigma\), explaining v3E's factor `26.46` at the middle mass.  It cannot make
that separation universal: both low-mass fixtures remain unscreened, while
both high-mass fixtures develop screened regions.  Changing the screen, memory
length, ordering, or scored volume after failure produces large variations but
does not establish a frozen universal law.

## Mechanism decision

The project will not test another algebraic scalar blend of v3D, v3E, and v3F.
No weighted sum, new exponent, moved threshold, or relaxed factor-ten gate is
authorized.

The v3E commutator remains a useful *tensor direction* clue, not an accepted
interaction.  The next mechanism class must be assessed through the
Euler--Lagrange field it produces rather than the volume integral of its scalar
Lagrangian density.

For a static weak-field probe, let

\[
\mathcal E_{ij}=D_{ij}W,
\qquad
D_{ij}=\partial_i\partial_j-\frac13\delta_{ij}\nabla^2,
\qquad
\mathcal M=H^{-1}\mathcal E,
\]

with `H=1-L_sigma^2 Laplacian`.  If `P_E` and `P_M` are the analytic tensor
derivatives of a candidate interaction, its fixed-screen Weyl source has the
schematic form

\[
\mathcal R_W
=D_{ij}\left[P_E^{ij}+H^{-1}P_M^{ij}\right].
\]

Unlike the scalar action-density score, `R_W` is signed, directional, and
directly capable of adding convergence in one region while subtracting it in
another.  It is closer to the field quantity that can create or destroy
critical curves.
