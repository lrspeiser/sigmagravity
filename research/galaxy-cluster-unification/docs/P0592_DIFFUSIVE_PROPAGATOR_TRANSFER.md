# P0592: a distribution of gravity-return paths

MACS J0416 strongly preferred a broadened baryon map, but a single displaced ring and its baryon-axis extension did not beat Gaussian smoothing. P0592 treats that Gaussian as an endpoint propagator: many hidden paths leave and return with a distribution of projected lengths.

The propagator is the heat kernel `exp[(ell^2/2) laplacian]`. It is positive and conservative. One universal dimensionless number sets `ell=q R80_b` for every cluster, and one universal fraction mixes propagated and local field weight. Seven clusters select the pair; three different clusters receive it unchanged. The same locked maps are also checked against all 100 Lenstool realizations and GLAFIC reconstructions.

This is an intentionally harsher transfer than MACS J0416 because these ten legacy baryon maps contain member light but omit the gas and ICL components that P0589 found important.
