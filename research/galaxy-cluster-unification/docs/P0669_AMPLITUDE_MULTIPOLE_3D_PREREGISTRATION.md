# P0669 amplitude-multipole 3D preregistration

P0668 showed that the measured baryonic multipoles separate registered
clusters from galaxies by more than two orders of magnitude in the final
coefficient, but the absolute cluster coefficient missed its frozen minimum.
P0669 does not relax that minimum or tune a new exponent.

Instead, it tests one specific interpretation of the earlier invariant:

\[
M=1-e^{-(D^2+Q^2)},\qquad A=\sqrt{M},\qquad
\sigma_{\rm final}=A\,\sigma_{\rm local}.
\]

If `M` is the fraction of routed field power or energy, the corresponding
nonnegative field-amplitude fraction is `sqrt(M)`. This is the same distinction
between adding amplitudes and comparing squared intensities that motivated the
vector-combination idea, expressed as a classical constitutive hypothesis. It
adds no constant and remains exactly zero when the stellar and gas centroids
and normalized quadrupoles agree.

Before any P0669 registered-map score is computed, the following remain
frozen:

- 13 registered galaxy and four registered cluster baryonic maps;
- the same three mass scenarios and common 33-cell cubic grid;
- galaxy median `sigma <= 0.001`;
- cluster median `sigma >= 0.001`;
- nominal cluster/galaxy ratio `>= 10`, and `>= 5` in every mass scenario;
- positive constitutive eigenvalues and conservative deprojection; and
- no new universal constant, no per-object gravity setting, and no target
  outcome opening.

A pass authorizes one spent-cluster map build. It is not evidence that the
candidate fits cluster lensing.
