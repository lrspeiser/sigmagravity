## Decision

The preferred-clock/ADM-trace route is closed under the project's
three-failure rule. Sigma v14A does **not** repair v13C and does not open any
observational data. It advances one genuinely different question to an action
gate:

> Can a gauge-reduced, zero-monopole tidal field carry baryon-registered
> orientation while the convex v13B scalar channel supplies the galaxy
> monopole, without creating an ordinary tensor ghost, material medium, hidden

This is a postulate selection, not a viable theory. No covariant v14A action
has yet passed a degree count, Hamiltonian reduction, characteristic test, or
weak-metric derivation. `theory_viable=false` and observations remain closed.

## Two new-language screens

### Ordinary p-forms do not supply a new four-dimensional carrier class

The local polarization counts for an Abelian p-form in four spacetime
dimensions are

$$
N_{\rm massless}=\binom{2}{p},
\qquad
N_{\rm massive}=\binom{3}{p}.
$$

Thus a massless two-form has one local polarization and is dual to a scalar;
a massive two-form has three and is dual to a massive vector; a massless
three-form has no local polarization. These fields return to scalar or vector
mechanisms already screened. Calling the carrier a Kalb--Ramond field would
not by itself add the required independent tidal representation.

### A direct higher-rank mass charge has the wrong Newtonian limit

A scalar-charge symmetric rank-two gauge field has a static scalar-potential
operator of fourth spatial order. In three dimensions,

$$
\nabla^4\varphi\propto\delta^3(\mathbf r)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\varphi\propto r,
\qquad |\nabla\varphi|\propto r^0.
$$

Newton instead requires

$$
\nabla^2\Phi\propto\delta^3(\mathbf r)
\quad\Longrightarrow\quad
\Phi\propto {1\over r},
\qquad |\nabla\Phi|\propto {1\over r^2}.
$$

Moreover, the scalar-charge gauge symmetry enforces dipole conservation, so
ordinary mobile baryonic mass cannot simply be declared its charge. The v14
tidal carrier is therefore forbidden from carrying the mass monopole. It may
be sourced only in a zero-monopole orientation channel whose conserved current
is derived from a covariant action.

Higher-rank gauge theories and longitudinal-diffeomorphism-like symmetric
tensor symmetries are established research, not a Sigma invention; examples
include [Moinuddin and Mukherjee](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.06636) and
[Fecit and Rovere](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.14591). Neither reference supplies
the gravity action proposed here. In particular, covariance alone does not
prove positive energy, acceptable matter sourcing, or a one-metric lensing
limit.

An algebraic metric-affine connection is also not the missing extended
carrier. Generic low-order metric-affine connections can be eliminated in
favor of local metric/matter interactions, and broad quadratic classes return
to GR outside hypermomentum sources; see
[Vitagliano, Sotiriou, and Liberati](https://arxiv.org/abs/1008.0171) and
[Iosifidis](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.13293). Making the connection propagate
does not evade the problem; it creates another field whose source, spectrum,
energy, and homogeneous state must be audited.

## Frozen v14A physical postulates

1. **One physical metric.** All baryons, massive tracers, clocks, and photons
   couple to one metric. There is no lensing metric or lens-only multiplier.
2. **Separated physical jobs, one action.** The v13B convex AQUAL-like response
   is retained only as the required monopole scaling target. A distinct
   zero-monopole tidal sector may carry orientation, but both must be derived
   from one covariant action and one metric equation.
3. **Gauge-reduced orientation.** The tidal carrier is represented by
   gauge-invariant field strengths of a symmetric trace-free response. It is
   not six unconstrained spacetime-tensor components and not an elastic or
   material triad.
4. **No direct mass charge.** Ordinary baryonic mass cannot be the charge of
   the higher-rank sector. A covariantly conserved, zero-monopole tidal source
   must follow from action variation.
5. **Baryon-unique state.** Fixed baryons and one universal retarded/no-incoming
   prescription must fix the physical state. No per-object homogeneous mode,
   center, orientation, scale, or amplitude is permitted.
6. **Hamiltonian before phenomenology.** The complete constrained Hamiltonian
   in gauge-invariant variables must be positive on arbitrary finite
   backgrounds before a weak-field solve is allowed.
7. **Weyl activity from the same metric.** The action must derive both
   `Psi` and `W=(Phi+Psi)/2`; the tidal response must affect the Weyl potential
   without a separate photon coupling.
8. **Structural GR limit.** The new channel vanishes in the high-field limit,
   leaves the physical tensor cone exactly luminal, and introduces no Solar
   cancellation tuned to an object.
9. **Four-constant reservation.** The provisional budget is
   `{a_sigma, epsilon, beta_sigma, L_sigma}`, leaving one slot unused. None may
   be fitted per galaxy or cluster.

The following placements are prohibited by the reset:

- khronon expansion or the ADM trace;
- an ordinary covariant component kinetic term for a spacetime tensor;
- a material-coordinate or elastic-triad strain;
- a localized retarded multiplier pair; and
- a direct rank-two gauge charge equal to baryonic mass.

## Binary action gates

V14A advances only if all of these are proved in order:

| Gate | Required result | Failure consequence |
|---|---|---|
| Covariant gauge definition | A four-dimensional gauge transformation, invariant field strengths, and complete action | reject a preferred-slice ansatz |
| Source compatibility | Action variation produces a conserved, zero-monopole tidal source built from the same baryonic/metric variables | reject a hand-written source |
| State uniqueness | Constraint analysis plus universal boundary data leave no object-level homogeneous profile | reject as hidden-halo-equivalent |
| Physical Hamiltonian | Every constraint-reduced kinetic and gradient eigenvalue is positive on arbitrary finite backgrounds | reject before weak fields |
| Characteristics | Every physical cone is on or inside the one-metric cone and `c_T=c` structurally | reject before Solar tests |
| One-metric weak limit | The action derives `Psi`, `Phi`, the v13B monopole window, and nonzero baryon-registered shear | only then write a solver |
| GR limit | High-field force, PPN, Mercury, and tensor-wave gates pass with the same constants | only then freeze evidence splits |

The first unresolved calculation is source compatibility. Known covariant
higher-rank symmetries often impose charge and dipole conservation on matter.
V14A must instead derive a neutral tidal current without secretly inserting a
dark profile. If that cannot be done while retaining diffeomorphism invariance
and one-metric matter conservation, this candidate is rejected immediately.
