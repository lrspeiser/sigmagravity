# P0676 spent RX J2129 transverse-confinement field preregistration

P0675 shows that a large coefficient is not enough when it suppresses the
constitutive eigenvalue along the inferred component-difference direction.
P0676 reverses the geometric interpretation without changing the activation:

\[
\mathbf A_{\rm confine}=\mu\left[(1-\sigma)\mathbf I+
\sigma\,\hat{\mathbf h}\hat{\mathbf h}\right].
\]

Along `h`, the eigenvalue is `mu`; in both perpendicular directions it is
`mu(1-sigma)`. This is a field-level analogue of a waveguide: the proposed
route remains open while transverse leakage is suppressed. It adds no sign,
amplitude, exponent, length, or per-object setting. The P0673 compound
coefficient, P0670 baryonic cube, simple-MOND boundary, nonlinear solver, and
zero-slip photon rule are unchanged.

Before solving, P0676 requires the new field to increase strong-lens RMS by at
least `20%` and differ vectorially from scalar by at least `20%`. It must also
converge below `1e-5`, remain finite and positive definite, preserve the exact
boundary, stay below a twentyfold numerical response, and remain curl-free.
No image or sealed target is scored unless every field gate passes.
