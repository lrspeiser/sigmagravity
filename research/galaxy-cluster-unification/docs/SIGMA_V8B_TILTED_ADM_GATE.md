# Sigma v8B tilted-ADM necessary kinetic gate

## Verdict

Sigma v8B passes a **bounded homogeneous local Legendre-rank subgate**, but it
does not pass the full Hamiltonian gate. An exact boundary subtraction removes
the apparent scalar normal acceleration on a tilted-aether homogeneous patch.
The resulting first-order ten-velocity Hessian remains full rank and preserves
its raw ADM inertia throughout 1,409 frozen scan points. However, a finite
rank-changing surface exists outside that declared envelope. The candidate is
therefore retained only for a full inhomogeneous Dirac and reachability audit;
observational fitting remains prohibited.

No observational array or held-out target was opened in this calculation.

## ADM reduction

Let $n^\mu$ be the ADM slice normal and write the unit timelike aether as

$$
A^\mu=\chi n^\mu+A^i,
\qquad
\chi=\sqrt{1+x},
\qquad
x=A_iA^i.
$$

For a locally homogeneous scalar, define

$$
\sigma=n^\mu\nabla_\mu\phi,
\qquad
Q=A^\mu\nabla_\mu\phi=\chi\sigma,
\qquad
B=Q-Q_0.
$$

With $K_A=K^{ij}A_iA_j$ and $A\!\cdot\!E=A^iE_i$, the spatially projected
Hessian becomes

$$
H_\perp=q^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\nabla_\nu\phi
=x\mathcal L_n\sigma-\sigma(K+K_A).
$$

The completion density is therefore

$$
\frac{\mathcal L_C}{\sqrt q}
=C B^2\left[x\mathcal L_n\sigma-\sigma(K+K_A)\right],
\qquad
C=(\alpha-1)L_H^2.
$$

At first sight this contains a second scalar time derivative. Introduce

$$
F(x,\sigma)=\frac{xB^3}{3\chi},
\qquad
F_{,\sigma}=xB^2,
\qquad
\mathcal L_n x=2(A\!\cdot\!E-K_A).
$$

Subtracting the exact density boundary term generated by $F$ gives

$$
\boxed{
\frac{\mathcal L_C^{(1)}}{\sqrt q}
=-C\left\{
K(F+\sigma B^2)
+K_A(\sigma B^2-2F_{,x})
+2F_{,x}A\!\cdot\!E
\right\}.
}
$$

The code verifies

$$
\mathcal L_C-\mathcal L_C^{(1)}
=C\sqrt q\left(\mathcal L_nF+KF\right)
$$

to an absolute residual of $2.71\times10^{-20}$ at the frozen audit point and
below $10^{-12}$ over randomized unit tests. Thus this patch requires no new
coordinate for $\mathcal L_n\sigma$.

$$

where $Y=x\sigma^2$ on this patch. The frozen simple AeST free function is
used without fitting. The complete local Hessian is taken with respect to

$$
(K_{xx},K_{yy},K_{zz},K_{xy},K_{xz},K_{yz},E_x,E_y,E_z,\sigma).

Its automatic-differentiation form agrees with an independent centered finite
difference at relative Frobenius error $5.37\times10^{-9}$ and maximum absolute

Full rank here means only that the local Legendre map is regular. If the usual
four diffeomorphism constraints survive, the patch has the same conditional
six physical degrees of freedom as the published AeST base. This is not a
Dirac constraint derivation. The raw Hessian inertia `(1 negative, 0 zero,
9 positive)` includes the familiar ADM conformal direction and is not itself a
physical ghost count.

## Finite surface outside the envelope

$$

\qquad

\qquad

\qquad

$$

the determinant crosses zero at

$$
\boxed{\frac{Q}{Q_0}=2.8649430865.}
$$

The minimum singular value there is $8.56\times10^{-13}$. Across the surface,
the raw inertia changes from `(1,0,9)` to `(2,0,8)`. The field values are
finite and $v_A<1$; the surface cannot be dismissed as a coordinate boundary.

This is the decisive warning. A consistent next stage must prove from the full
constraints and evolution that physical solutions cannot reach this surface.
Merely declaring the scanned envelope to be the effective-theory domain would
be insufficient unless the field equations enforce that domain.

## Decision and next kill gate
