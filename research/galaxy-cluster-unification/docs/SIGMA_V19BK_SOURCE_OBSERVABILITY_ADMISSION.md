# Sigma V19BK source-observability admission

## Result

The registered data do not presently support all five V19BJ source candidates.
Before any regional gas fit or invariant score exists, V19BK narrows the
scientifically eligible calculation to:

- **I4 thermodynamic-gradient stress:** an eligible projected scalar amplitude
  and 2D axial tensor direction; and
- **I5 baroclinicity:** an eligible scalar activation only.

I1, I2, I3 and I6 are withheld rather than completed with assumed data.

## Why four candidates are withheld

The member maps contain relative Bessel-I luminosity in the Bullet Cluster and
relative F814W luminosity in Abell 2146. They are excellent morphology tracers,
but they are not calibrated stellar masses in common units. Component overlap
$4f_g(1-f_g)$ therefore cannot be calculated without an invented
mass-to-light conversion.

The member products contain line-of-sight current and one line-of-sight second
moment. They explicitly contain no east/north velocities. The gas posterior
will contain sound speed but no directly measured gas velocity vector. Thus a
relative-current vector and a full anisotropic-stress tensor are not
identified. A single snapshot also supplies no material time derivative.

The automatic front route is paused. V19J confused smooth gradients with
discontinuities; V19K and V19L failed mandatory pre-science fixtures. V19L did
not read either cluster science array. A fourth detector will not be tuned
while the gas-gradient candidates are being evaluated.

## Stricter density control

The required “not just density” test is strengthened. At each adaptive gas
region, the fixed quadratic nuisance model receives:

- log gas surface density;
- within-cluster stellar-light percentile rank;
- gas-density gradient magnitude;
- gas-density Hessian trace; and
- gas-density Hessian anisotropy.

It also receives every quadratic term and pairwise interaction. Analytic
leave-one-region-out residuals determine the cross-validated unexplained
variance. At least 20% must remain. This prevents a candidate from appearing
novel merely because it differentiates density, while avoiding a false
cross-filter stellar-mass comparison.

## What I4 and I5 can establish

I4 can test whether entropy and density gradients form a stable projected
tensor whose amplitude and axis survive posterior, resolution and
leave-one-region-out perturbations in both clusters. I5 can test whether
pressure and density gradients have a stable nonzero misalignment not
predicted by the density controls.

Neither result by itself is gravity. A pass only identifies a baryonic source
state worth deriving from a covariant action. Lensing remains sealed until that
derivation fixes the field content, equations and universal constants.
