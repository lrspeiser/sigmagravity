## One physical metric and the dust-cancelling limit

Start from the previously derived reciprocal scalar-vector metric,

\[
\widetilde g_{\mu\nu}=e^{2\alpha X}
\left(g_{\mu\nu}+2\beta XU_\mu U_\nu\right).
\]

Varying the same matter action that determines motion gives

\[
J_X=\alpha T+\beta E,
\qquad
(\Box-L_X^{-2})X=-{J_X\over M_{\rm Pl}^2}.
\]

The structurally interesting choice is not an object switch. It is the single
universal relation \(\alpha=\beta\). Cold rest mass then cancels because
\(T\simeq-\rho c^2\) and \(E\simeq\rho c^2\). For an isotropic fluid,

\[
\boxed{J_X=3\alpha p.}
\]

The same physical metric fixes both weak-field potentials:

\[
\Psi=U_N,
\qquad
\Phi=U_N-c^2\alpha X,
\qquad
W={\Phi+\Psi\over2}=U_N-{c^2\alpha X\over2}.
\]

This extra channel is invisible to slow-particle dynamics at linear order but
not to light. That is not a hand-added lensing multiplier: it follows from one
metric. It is also established conformal-disformal/TeVeS-like prior art; the
project-specific hypothesis is the measured pressure source and its universal
cross-cluster transfer.

## The reciprocal bound

Define a dimensionless pressure compactness,

\[
\Pi_p={\int 3p\,dV\over Mc^2}.
\]

In the most favorable unsuppressed exterior limit, the extra Weyl field and the
Solar PPN slip are

\[
f_W=\alpha^2\Pi_p,
\qquad
|\gamma-1|=2\alpha^2\Pi_p.
\]

For 17 keV cluster gas with \(\mu=0.61\),

\[
\Pi_{\rm cluster}=8.91\times10^{-5}.
\]

Requiring only \(f_W=1\) needs \(\alpha=105.94\). For the Sun we deliberately
use the low virial estimate

\[
\Pi_\odot=0.5{GM_\odot\over R_\odot c^2}=1.0613\times10^{-6}.
\]

The cluster-strength coupling then predicts

\[
|\gamma-1|_\odot=0.0238204,
\]

which is 1,036 times the Cassini bound \(2.3\times10^{-5}\). Looking from the
other direction, the largest Cassini-safe coupling supplies only

\[
f_{W,{\rm cluster}}=9.6556\times10^{-4},
\]

less than one tenth of one percent of the deliberately modest cluster target.
The mismatch is independent of \(\alpha\), so changing the universal coupling
cannot fix it.

These assumptions favor the candidate: the hottest gas is used, every baryon
is treated as if it carries that temperature, finite-range attenuation is
ignored, and the required extra cluster field is only order unity. A realistic
gas fraction or larger baryon-to-lensing gap makes the obstruction stronger.

\[
