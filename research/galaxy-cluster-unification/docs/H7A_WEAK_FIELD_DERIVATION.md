## Action and variation

Let $X$ be an environment field held fixed when varying the nonrelativistic
potential $\Psi$. Define

$$
Y=\frac{|\nabla\Psi|^2}{a_X^2(X)}
$$

and the action

$$
S_{\rm wf}=\int dt\,d^3x\left[
-\frac{a_X^2(X)}{8\pi G}\mathcal F(Y)
-\rho_b\Psi+\mathcal L_X
\right].
$$

Variation with respect to $\Psi$ gives

$$
\nabla\cdot\left[\mathcal F_Y(Y)\nabla\Psi\right]
=4\pi G\rho_b.
$$

For the first closure choose

$$
x=\sqrt{Y},\qquad
\mu(x)=\mathcal F_Y=\frac{x}{1+x},
$$

which is generated by

$$
\mathcal F(Y)=Y-2\sqrt{Y}+2\ln(1+\sqrt{Y}).
$$

Thus the interpolation is derived from an explicit weak-field action rather
than applied only as an acceleration mapping. In spherical symmetry,

$$
g_{\rm bar}=\mu(g/a_X)g
$$

has the positive solution

$$
g=\frac{g_{\rm bar}+
\sqrt{g_{\rm bar}^2+4a_Xg_{\rm bar}}}{2}.
$$

It approaches $g\simeq\sqrt{a_Xg_{\rm bar}}$ in the deep limit and
$g\simeq g_{\rm bar}+a_X$ in the high-field limit.
