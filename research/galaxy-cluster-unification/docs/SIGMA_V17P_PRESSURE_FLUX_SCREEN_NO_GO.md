# Sigma v17P pressure flux-screen no-go

## Question

V17N proved that a decreasing acceleration susceptibility cannot safely sit
inside the derivative-dependent physical matter metric. The clean alternative
is to leave the physical metric non-derivative and put the screen in the
pressure scalar's own kinetic flux:

\[
\nabla\!\cdot[\mu(t)\mathbf g_\Sigma]
=4\pi G\alpha^2{3p\over c^2},
\qquad t={|\mathbf g_\Sigma|\over a_\Sigma}.
\]

This is an AQUAL/K-mouflage-like mechanism. It is established prior art, not a
new class of field theory. The project-specific question is whether it can
rescue the dust-cancelling pressure channel while keeping one physical metric.

## Why it initially looks promising

For a compact pressure source, the nonlinear radius would be

\[
r_K=\alpha\sqrt{{G\over a_\Sigma}
\int {3p\over c^2}\,dV}.
\]

That is the kind of halo-size prediction we seek: integrated baryonic random
stress determines a scale, while the full pressure map determines shape and
orientation. There is no fitted halo radius and no galaxy/cluster label.

Moreover, a growing \(\mu(t)\) can make the local scalar force extremely small
near the Sun even when the coupling is large enough for a cluster.

## The conserved-charge obstruction

Define

\[
\Pi_p={\int3p\,dV\over Mc^2},\qquad
x={GM\over a_\Sigma r^2},\qquad
y={r\over\sqrt{GM/a_\Sigma}}.
\]

Spherical flux conservation outside the baryons gives

\[
\mu(t)t={\alpha^2\Pi_p\over y^2}.
\]

The spent CLASH diagnostic points all require more than an order-unity extra
field and have \(x\ge0.109\). We conservatively require only one extra baryonic
field at \(x_c=0.1\). Let

\[
R={\Pi_\odot\over\Pi_{\rm cluster}}.
\]

For every positive monotone screen with \(\mu(0)=1\), the radius where the
Solar scalar field falls to \(t=x_c\) obeys

\[
y_c^2\ge {R\over x_c}.
\]

Outside that radius the screen is weaker, not stronger, and the conserved
source implies

\[
t(y)\ge {R\over y^2}.
\]

The outer field therefore supplies a minimum potential at an observer
\(y_{\rm obs}\):

\[
{U_\Sigma\over U_N}
=y_{\rm obs}\int_{y_{\rm obs}}^\infty t(y)\,dy
\ge y_{\rm obs}\sqrt{Rx_c}.
\]

For the reciprocal physical metric inherited from v17G,

\[
|\gamma-1|_{\rm proxy}\ge2y_{\rm obs}\sqrt{Rx_c}.
\]

This is the decisive quantity. Screening the force close to the Sun does not
erase the outer \(1/r\) potential generated by the conserved scalar charge.

## Frozen audit

The executable audit uses the exact v17G pressure compactness values, the
conservative 10 AU control radius, and the spent CLASH acceleration floor. It
verifies the theorem with

\[
\mu_n(t)=1+t^n,\qquad n=2,4,6,8,
\]

at cluster accelerations \(x_c=0.1,0.3,1\). These curves are numerical

the mechanism class.

## Result

All 84 spent CLASH diagnostic points require an extra field larger than the
baryonic field, and their minimum acceleration is

\[
{g_{\rm bar}\over a_\Sigma}=0.10910.
\]

Using the still more favorable frozen floor (x_c=0.1), the exact v17G
pressure compactness ratio is

\[
R={\Pi_\odot\over\Pi_{\rm cluster}}=0.0119102.
\]

At 10 AU, (y_{\rm obs}=0.00142250), so the theorem gives

\[
\boxed{|\gamma-1|_{\rm proxy}\ge9.8185\times10^{-5}}.
\]

That is 4.269 times the (2.3\times10^{-5}) Cassini limit. The Solar field
cannot even fall to the conservative cluster threshold (t=0.1) until at
least 2,426 AU. A universal 12.5 kpc or longer cluster propagation range is
effectively infinite on this scale and cannot remove the tail.

The 12 explicit witnesses verify the analytic bound. Across
(n=2,4,6,8) and (x_c=0.1,0.3,1), the smallest full-potential proxy is

\[
|\gamma-1|_{\rm proxy}=6.7274\times10^{-4},
\]

or 29.25 Cassini limits. The maximum normalized flux-equation residual is
(2.82\times10^{-14}). Some steep curves make the local-force proxy pass,
which demonstrates why scoring only the force would have produced a false
positive; their conserved outer potential still fails.

The frozen outcome is
`retire_monotone_shift_symmetric_pressure_flux_screen`. Changing the power or
smooth shape of a monotone kinetic curve is not an authorized follow-up.

This result does not reject every pressure source. A materially different
successor would have to change the integrated Solar scalar charge—for example
through a healthy potential-dependent source screen—or else reset the direct
pressure-channel premise. Such a successor must be evaluated as a complete
reciprocal field theory; a hand-suppressed Solar source is not sufficient.
