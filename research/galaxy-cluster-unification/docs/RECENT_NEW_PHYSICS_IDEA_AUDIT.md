# Recent new-physics idea disposition audit

## Bottom line

The recent discussion was grouped into 20 scientifically distinct idea
families rather than counting every rewording or parameter change as a new
idea.

- 14/20 received a direct empirical test on at least one relevant dataset.
- 4/20 received only a proxy or incomplete test.
- 2/20 did not receive a test of the prediction that made the idea genuinely
  different from standard physics.

Strictly, those last two ideas were narrowed mainly by inherited theoretical
assumptions. More broadly, 6/20 were not fully tested. Three of those six were
at times described more conclusively than the available evidence justified.
This audit corrects their status below.

Neither prior art nor disagreement with GR, QED, MOND, or another accepted
framework is a rejection criterion in this project. Those theories supply
controls, existing observations, and possible consistency checks. A proposed
mechanism is retired only at the level actually tested by data.

## Where the language outran the evidence

Three idea families need explicit correction.

1. A uniform void influence was dismissed using the standard common-
   acceleration argument. That argument is valid for universal coupling, but
   the proposed new physics could make coupling depend on radius, field state,
   coherence, density, or path history. Such a differential law was not ruled
   out by the argument.
2. The causal catch-up calculation showed that one time-derivative completion
   is invisible in equilibrium data. It did not show that all delayed,
   history-dependent, or incomplete-summation laws fail.
3. Quantum-branch and non-space ideas were classified as speculation because
   standard QED and GR do not supply the proposed mechanism. That is a prior-
   theory observation, not an empirical rejection of a new mechanism.

## Data-first disposition rule

Every idea now receives one of five labels:

1. **Untested** — no forward equation and distinctive observable yet.
2. **Proxy-tested** — a simplification was tested; the parent idea remains
   open.
3. **Directly tested in a stated range** — the distinctive mechanism was
   varied against relevant data.
4. **Disfavored in the tested range** — it predicts the data worse or fails a
   frozen holdout/consistency gate.
5. **Empirically unidentified** — the available data cannot distinguish the
   parameter from zero or from another term.

No future report should say that an entire idea is “ruled out” when only a
proxy, one parameterization, or one parameter range failed. Literature is used
to identify prior art and existing measurements, never as a substitute for the
project's own forward test.

Before a formula family is retired, the project should:

- state the distinctive observable that separates it from the control;
- test a baseline plus small changes in amplitude, scale, exponent, direction,
  placement, and saturation when those knobs are meaningful;
- transfer one universal setting to galaxy, cluster-lensing, and Solar-System
  data, while adding a mechanism-specific dataset when static data are
  insensitive;
- report both improvements and failures without converting a proxy failure
  into a family-wide rejection; and
- leave the status as untested or unidentified when the necessary data do not
  exist.

## Reopened empirical targets

The two untested mechanisms and the causal proxy require different data from
ordinary equilibrium rotation curves:

- **Differential uniform-void coupling:** compare matched galaxies with
  measured three-dimensional environment vectors and resolved outer-disk
  kinematics, testing whether residual acceleration follows the external-field
  direction after baryonic structure is controlled.
- **Causal/history response:** use merging clusters or other evolving systems
  with lensing, gas, galaxy, and velocity maps; static lensing alone cannot
  identify a time-response term.
- **Quantum/path-dependent photon gravity:** formulate a branch- or
  coherence-dependent phase prediction and test interferometric phase,
  wavelength, polarization, path-separation, and gravitational-potential
  scaling. Galaxy photometry alone cannot isolate this mechanism.
