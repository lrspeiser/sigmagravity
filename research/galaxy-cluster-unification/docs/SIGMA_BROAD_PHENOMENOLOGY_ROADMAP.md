# Sigma Gravity broad-phenomenology roadmap

## Present priority

The project should optimize against diverse galaxies and clusters first. The
Solar System remains a mandatory later veto and high-field-limit check, but it
must not become a tuning dataset while the theory still lacks the spatial
structure required by cluster lensing.

The central question is whether one baryon-sourced field can predict both
ordered disks and multi-component merging clusters without an object label or
per-object force parameter. Aggregate error is insufficient: performance must
be stratified by the observable geometries that could reveal the mechanism.

## Tier 1: mechanism selection now

| Domain | Required stratification | What it discriminates |
|---|---|---|
| Galaxy rotation curves | dwarf/giant, gas fraction, surface brightness, bulge fraction, scale length, concentration, asymmetry and environment | acceleration-only law versus distribution- or morphology-sensitive field |
| Raw strong lensing | relaxed/merging, one/multiple baryonic peaks, image family, radius, azimuth, multiplicity and critical-curve topology | amplitude correction versus correct two-dimensional curvature |
| Cluster baryon maps | gas, BCG, intracluster light, members, component overlap, pressure, entropy, shocks and relative currents | density source versus stress/current/nonlocal source |
| Joint systems | dynamics and lensing from the same metric and constants | prevents unrelated matter and photon fixes |
| Merger offsets | gas-member-front separations and orientations, scored before lensing | whether the field naturally occupies locations usually assigned to displaced dark halos |

The immediate V19 pipeline addresses the last three rows on the spent Bullet
Cluster and Abell 2146 pair before any untouched cluster is opened.

## Tier 2: consequences if one invariant survives

Freeze the action and constants, then predict with no new gravity parameters:

- weak-lensing reduced shear and magnification, including angular structure;
- flexion and strong-lensing time delays, which probe higher derivatives and
  the potential rather than only deflection;
- BCG, satellite and intracluster-light dynamics;
- galaxy-galaxy lensing and satellite planes across host mass;
- dwarf-spheroidal dispersions and tidal streams;
- escape-speed profiles and dynamical friction; and
- colliding-cluster shock/member timing.

These are not interchangeable. A theory can match a radial rotation curve yet
fail shear orientation, image multiplicity, time delay, or satellite dynamics.
That pattern would identify which part of the field equation is wrong.

## Tier 3: dark-matter-attributed cosmology

A local galaxy/cluster success would still not replace dark matter. The same
covariant action must eventually predict:

- linear growth and the matter power spectrum;
- CMB acoustic-peak positions and relative heights;
- BAO and redshift-space distortions;
- cosmic shear and cluster abundance;
- early structure formation and high-redshift galaxies; and
- the background expansion without reinterpreting fitted cosmological
  distances as direct evidence for the candidate.

These tests require perturbing the final action around a cosmological
background. They should not be attempted with the current empirical bridge,
because an interpolation formula has no unique perturbation, energy density,
or propagation law.

## Solar-System role

For now, require an explicit path to the GR/Newtonian limit and reject obvious
unscreened high-field behavior. Perform the expensive PPN, ephemeris and
stability suite only after a candidate passes the diverse galaxy and cluster
mechanism gates. Final acceptance still requires the full Cassini, Mercury,
wave-speed, positivity, causality and well-posedness checks.
