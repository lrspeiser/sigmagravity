# Sigma v16 boundary falsification and v17 dynamical-stress gate

## Decision

The measured static outer-baryon field does **not** supply the missing cluster
Weyl structure with one transferable operator. The authoritative nested test
improves the symmetric cross-cluster full-field NRMSE from `0.817941` to
`0.813831`, only `0.5025%` against the frozen `10%` advancement gate. It also
fails the required shear-alignment, boundary-power, and absolute-error gates.

The result closes the tested instantaneous static family: local density,
gradients, Hessians, gas--star overlap, finite-window harmonic boundary modes,
and measured baryons in four annuli from 200 to 1,800 kpc. It does not rule out
unmeasured line-of-sight matter, causal history, or baryonic dynamical stress.
No holdout was opened and no physical constant was fitted.

The original v16 protocol scored a 275 kpc disk while tapering the missing

The replacement passes its declared separation and padding-integrity checks.

## What was decomposed

For a convergence field inside a finite measurement region, the E-mode shear
generated by that convergence is fixed by the one-potential relation

\[
\widehat\gamma_1 =
{k_E^2-k_N^2\over k_E^2+k_N^2}\widehat\kappa,
\qquad
\widehat\gamma_2 =
{2k_Ek_N\over k_E^2+k_N^2}\widehat\kappa.
\]

Subtracting this internal field from the missing target shear defines a
diagnostic boundary remainder. An analytic potential generated outside the
disk is harmonic inside it. In complex coordinates its regular modes satisfy

\[
\psi_m \propto \operatorname{Re}(z^m),\operatorname{Im}(z^m),
\qquad m\ge 2,
\]

and produce zero convergence but spatially structured shear. Target-derived
harmonic fits are an oracle upper bound, never model parameters.

Measured baryons outside 200 kpc were independently converted into exact
one-potential shear fields in the annuli 200--500, 500--900, 900--1,300, and
1,300--1,800 kpc. The same real coefficient multiplies convergence and both
shear components. No cluster receives a private coefficient, orientation, or
external-shear term.

## Physical implication

The failure says more than "the amplitude is wrong." The measured static
baryons do not predict where and in which orientation the missing Hessian must
appear in another cluster. Adding more radial multipliers to density is
therefore not justified by these maps.

The next source question is whether gravity responds to the full coarse-grained
baryonic stress-energy tensor rather than only its rest-density component. In
the local Landau frame, write

\[
T_b^{\mu\nu}u_\nu=-\rho_b c^2 u^\mu,
\qquad
h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+u_\mu u_\nu,
\]

and define the spatial stress

\[
S_{\mu\nu}=h_\mu{}^\alpha h_\nu{}^\beta T^b_{\alpha\beta},
\qquad
\Theta_b={\sqrt{S_{\mu\nu}S^{\mu\nu}}\over \rho_b c^2}.
\]

A cold coherent stream has nearly zero spatial stress in its own Landau frame.
A hot gas or unresolved collection of crossing collisionless streams does not.
This provides an object-label-free distinction between a cold rotating disk
and a merging cluster. It is only a source postulate: a viable theory must
derive the response from a healthy universally coupled action and must generate
its own coarse-graining scale.
