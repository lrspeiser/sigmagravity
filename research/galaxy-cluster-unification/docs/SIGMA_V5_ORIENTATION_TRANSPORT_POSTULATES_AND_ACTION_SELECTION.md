   couple minimally to the same metric \(g_{ab}\).
2. **No invisible material source.** Observed baryonic stress energy is the
   only material source. A Sigma response belongs to the gravitational action,

   per object.
3. **Baryon-unique state.** For fixed baryons and one universal early-time or
   decaying boundary prescription, every trace/orientation state is unique.
   Adding a homogeneous solution is prohibited.
4. **Two geometric jobs.** A sourced trace state supplies broad positive
   curvature; an orientation-preserving transport operator retains separated
   baryonic multipoles. Neither job may be replaced by an object label.
5. **Nonlinear onset.** The new channel vanishes in the GR vacuum quadratic
   action, in a coherent field, and in the high-acceleration limit. This keeps
   the ordinary local graviton cone and Solar-System behavior as structural
   limits rather than fitted cancellations.
6. **Causal memory.** The four-dimensional response is retarded. A static
   Helmholtz inverse is permitted only as the stationary limit of a causal
   equation or of a genuinely constrained, degenerate system.
7. **Energy accounting.** The complete metric/memory equations must obey the
   diffeomorphism Noether identity. Any gravitational effective stress is
   derived and reported; it is not silently discarded or renamed baryons.
8. **Universal economy.** At most five universal constants are permitted. No
   galaxy/cluster switch, lensing-only amplitude, per-object scale,
   orientation, center, or shear is permitted.

## Why three obvious couplings are not equivalent

### 1. A plain scalar--curvature factor does not enhance light

Consider

\[
S\supset {M_P^2\over2}\int\sqrt{-g}\,F(\Sigma)R.
\]

Ignoring scalar stress at leading weak order, its metric equation is

\[
F G_{ab}+(g_{ab}\Box-\nabla_a\nabla_b)F=8\pi G T_{ab}.
\]

For

\[
ds^2=-(1+2\Psi/c^2)c^2dt^2+(1-2\Phi/c^2)d\mathbf x^2,
\]

the scalar contribution shifts the two Laplacians oppositely,

\[
\nabla^2\Phi=4\pi G\rho_b+{1\over2}\nabla^2\delta F,
\qquad
\nabla^2\Psi=4\pi G\rho_b-{1\over2}\nabla^2\delta F.
\]

Therefore

\[
\nabla^2W
={1\over2}\nabla^2(\Phi+\Psi)
=4\pi G\rho_b.
\]

The force on massive matter changes, but the linear scalar contribution
cancels from lensing. `F(Sigma) R` alone cannot be the missing cluster channel.

### 2. A generic tensor--Weyl coupling threatens the graviton cone

A term such as

\[
M^{ab}C_{acbd}u^cu^d
\]

does carry orientation and can affect shear directly. On a background with
nonzero \(M^{ab}\), however, it changes the principal tensor operator unless a
specific degeneracy removes that change. Merely choosing a small coefficient
is not the structural requirement \(c_T=c\). An unconstrained \(M^{ab}\) also
has a homogeneous profile capable of acting as a hidden halo. This coupling is
not selected without a full degeneracy proof.

### 3. A Weyl-selecting nonmetricity trace is a safer action direction

In symmetric teleparallel geometry define

\[
Q_a=Q_{a\ m}{}^m,
\qquad
\widetilde Q_a=Q^m{}_{am},
\qquad
\mathcal W_a=Q_a-4\widetilde Q_a.
\]

For the static scalar metric in three spatial dimensions,

\[
Q_i=2\partial_i\Psi-6\partial_i\Phi,
\qquad
\widetilde Q_i=-2\partial_i\Phi,
\]

so

\[
\boxed{\mathcal W_i=2\partial_i(\Psi+\Phi)=4\partial_iW.}
\]

Consequently

\[
\boxed{
\mathbb W=\mathcal W_a\mathcal W^a
\longrightarrow {16\over c^4}|\nabla W|^2
}
\]

at weak static order. A coefficient multiplying \(\mathbb W\) therefore acts
on the physical photon potential rather than only on \(\Psi-\Phi\).

Both nonmetricity traces vanish for a linear transverse-traceless metric
perturbation. A nonlinear coefficient times \(\mathbb W\) therefore leaves the
vacuum quadratic TT action unchanged. This is a strong `c_T` screening clue,
not yet a complete mode proof on arbitrary backgrounds.

## The unresolved action problem

A retarded inverse cannot be obtained by varying an ordinary real, single-copy
nonlocal action: ordinary variation produces a time-symmetric kernel. Two
honest routes remain:

1. a closed-time-path/in-in effective action whose physical-limit equation is
   explicitly retarded; or
2. a local degenerate constraint system whose auxiliary state has no
   independent Cauchy data and whose stationary reduction is the displayed
   anisotropic operator.

Naively localizing the inverse with two ordinary auxiliaries is prohibited; it
generically creates one positive and one negative cross-kinetic eigenmode.
Likewise, simply declaring the homogeneous solution zero is a boundary
condition, not a proof that a fundamental propagating field cannot store a
halo-like state.
