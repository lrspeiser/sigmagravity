# P0593: cluster diffusion in galaxies and the Solar System

This stage applies the cluster-locked `ell=0.5 R80`, fully routed conservative propagator to the force-equivalent baryonic mass profiles of 131 SPARC galaxies. It also varies four small ingredients: broadening versus projected contraction, spatial strength, a source-level low-acceleration gate, and the presence or absence of a fixed empirical RAR scalar completion.

The central structural test is whether conservative redistribution alone can create the missing outer-galaxy force. It cannot increase the force outside a surface enclosing all routed source mass. Contraction can move mass inside an observed radius and broadening can move it outside, but neither changes the asymptotic monopole.

For a finite spherical Sun, every tested redistribution scale is below one solar radius. The conservative exterior force at planetary distances is unchanged up to an exponentially negligible Gaussian tail. The empirical RAR completion is also numerically zero at planetary accelerations. This is a force-level audit only: no Cassini/PPN claim is possible before specifying the relativistic metric.
