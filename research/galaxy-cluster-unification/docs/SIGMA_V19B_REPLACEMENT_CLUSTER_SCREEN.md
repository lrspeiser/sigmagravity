# Sigma v19B target-blind replacement-cluster screen

## Result

The Bullet Cluster and Abell 2146 are selected as a **mechanism-development
pair** for the causal-assembly investigation. Selection used only source-side
shock, member, gas, and merger-geometry evidence. Their multiple-image
coordinates, lens models, inferred halo maps, and lensing scores remain sealed.

This result authorizes reproducible acquisition of source-side data. It does
not authorize construction of a causal Sigma source, formula selection, or any
lensing test.

## Why these two survived

| Source-side criterion | Bullet Cluster | Abell 2146 |
|---|---:|---:|
| Secure spectroscopic members | 78 | 63 |
| Direct primary-merger front | yes | yes, two shocks |
| Conservative front statistic | 6.83 sigma | 6.50 sigma |
| Published projection constraint | 5--15 deg from sky plane | 13--19 deg from sky plane |
| Published time-since-passage interval | 0.10--0.20 Gyr | 0.24--0.28 Gyr |
| Public member and Chandra source data identified | yes | yes |
| Source-construction uncertainty bundle local and hashed | no | no |

For the Bullet Cluster, the conservative statistic is the Chandra
`M_X=2.57+/-0.23` result quoted in the same electron-heating comparison as the
ALMA+ACA analysis. The corresponding significance against the no-shock
boundary `M=1` is `(2.57-1)/0.23=6.83 sigma`. The older Chandra result also
reports a prominent `M=3.0+/-0.4` bow shock. Barrena et al. report 78 secure
members, a 5--15 degree sky-plane projection, and a collision age near
`0.15+/-0.05 Gyr`.

For Abell 2146, the deeper 400 ks Chandra observation gives a bow-shock Mach
number `M=2.3+/-0.2`, or `6.50 sigma` above `M=1`, and an upstream shock
`M=1.6+/-0.1`. The later 2 Ms observation resolves the bow and upstream shock
widths. White et al. report 63 spectroscopic members, a 13--19 degree
projection, and 0.24--0.28 Gyr since first passage.

These significances establish real propagation fronts. They do **not** remove
the physical assumptions used to turn a front and projected component
separation into time since core passage. The acquisition stage must propagate
shock/subcluster-speed, inclination, impact-parameter, gas-geometry, and
electron-heating uncertainties rather than treating either published age as an
exact clock.

## Why the alternatives did not survive

El Gordo is an excellent later lensing object and has 89 reported
spectroscopic members, but the source-side ALMA shock detection was reported
above 98 percent confidence, only about 2.05 one-sided Gaussian sigma. Its
joint Mach estimate is broad, and its dynamical reconstruction retains
outgoing and returning ages near 0.46 and 0.91 Gyr. It therefore fails the
frozen five-sigma common-clock screen.

MACS J0025.4-1222 has a compelling gas--galaxy separation and public lensing
data, but the screened primary source does not provide a directly measured
primary-merger shock clock, a quantified projection ensemble, or the required
member uncertainty table. It is not a substitute for either selected object.

## Development pair is not the final lensing sample

The Bullet Cluster has ample later target metadata: 14 reported multiply
imaged systems and three spectroscopic families. Abell 2146 has four
conservative systems, but the source reports no spectroscopically confirmed
lensed family. Abell 2146 therefore cannot currently satisfy the project's
final raw-lensing sample gate. It is still valuable for source-mechanism
development because it has unusually deep Chandra data and a clean two-shock
geometry.

This distinction prevents us from weakening the final evidence standard merely
to retain a physically useful development object.

## Exact next gate

Before any causal source is built, both selected clusters must have:

1. a local, parsed member table retaining position, redshift or velocity, and
   measurement errors;
2. a front position and normal with uncertainties measured on registered
   Chandra products;
3. resolved gas density and temperature uncertainty realizations;
4. a reproducible 3D projection ensemble covering the published geometric and
   clock assumptions; and
5. hashes and provenance for every source-side input.

Only then may one universal source construction be frozen. The lensing
coordinates must remain sealed until that freeze. Apparent halo extent,
centroid, and orientation will be outputs computed from the resulting metric,
not inputs copied from a dark-matter reconstruction.
