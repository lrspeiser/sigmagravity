# One-metric action constraints for Sigma Gravity

## Result established before the v17 lensing score

A minimally healthy, conformally coupled scalar can add a force to massive
particles, but its **direct** weak-field contribution cancels from the potential
that bends light. This is an action-level reason that a galaxy-strength scalar
force does not automatically solve cluster lensing. The cancellation follows
from one universal physical metric; it is not an assumption that photons obey a
separate law.

This memo is deliberately prior to the v17 thermal-stress target opening. It
does not choose an action from the cluster result and does not introduce a
galaxy/cluster switch.

## The minimal scalar starting point

Consider the broad one-scalar action

\[
S=\int d^4x\sqrt{-g}\left[
{M_{\rm Pl}^2\over2}R+M^4K(X/M^4)
\right]
+S_m[\widetilde g_{\mu\nu},\psi_m],
\qquad
X=-{1\over2}g^{\mu\nu}\nabla_\mu\sigma\nabla_\nu\sigma,
\]

with one physical metric for every matter field and photon,

\[
\widetilde g_{\mu\nu}=A^2(\sigma/M_{\rm Pl})g_{\mu\nu}.
\]

The function \(K\) can provide nonlinear kinetic screening, while \(A\) supplies
a universal scalar coupling. No object label appears. With ordinary
Einstein--Hilbert tensor dynamics and a first-derivative scalar Lagrangian, the
tensor characteristic cone is the metric null cone. A conformal transformation
does not change null cones, so gravitational waves and photons have the same
local propagation speed in this minimal class.

Diffeomorphism invariance gives conservation of the total metric-plus-scalar-
matter stress. Matter is covariantly conserved with respect to the physical
metric and freely falling bodies follow its geodesics. In the Einstein-frame
variables, matter and the scalar exchange stress; that bookkeeping exchange is
not a failure of total conservation.

For \(\alpha=d\ln A/d(\sigma/M_{\rm Pl})\), the scalar equation is schematically

\[
\nabla_\mu\left(K_X\nabla^\mu\sigma\right)
=-{\alpha\over M_{\rm Pl}}T,
\]

where \(T\) is the matter stress-energy trace. This is the standard scalar-
tensor source structure, not a new Sigma result.

## Weak-field derivation for mass and light

Write the Einstein-frame metric as

\[
ds^2=-(1+2\Psi_E)c^2dt^2+(1-2\Phi_E)d\mathbf{x}^2
\]

and linearize

\[
A(\sigma/M_{\rm Pl})\simeq1+{\alpha\sigma\over M_{\rm Pl}}.
\]

The physical metric then contains

\[
\widetilde\Psi=\Psi_E+{\alpha\sigma\over M_{\rm Pl}},
\qquad
\widetilde\Phi=\Phi_E-{\alpha\sigma\over M_{\rm Pl}}.
\]

A slow massive particle responds to

\[
\mathbf a=-\nabla\widetilde\Psi
=-\nabla\Psi_E-{\alpha\over M_{\rm Pl}}\nabla\sigma.
\]

The scalar therefore can strengthen a rotation curve. Light responds to the
Weyl or lensing potential

\[
W={\widetilde\Phi+\widetilde\Psi\over2}
={\Phi_E+\Psi_E\over2}.
\]

The direct linear scalar term cancels exactly. The scalar can still bend light
indirectly through its own stress-energy in the Einstein equations, but that
stress is quadratic in weak scalar gradients. It is normally much smaller than
the linear fifth force. Making it cluster-sized is not impossible, but it must
be demonstrated without creating excessive field energy, an instability, or a
Solar-System signal.

This rules out a large class of superficially elegant proposals: adding a
conformal scalar force to Newtonian gravity is not by itself a unified
galaxy-and-lensing theory.

## Why ordinary pressure is not yet enough

For an approximately isotropic fluid in the \((-+++ )\) convention,

\[
T=-\rho c^2+3p.
\]

For intracluster plasma,

\[
{p\over\rho c^2}\sim{k_BT\over\mu m_pc^2}
\sim1.7\times10^{-5}\left({T\over10\ {\rm keV}}\right).
\]

Thus a trace-coupled scalar sees the gas temperature only as a correction of a
few parts in \(10^5\) to the rest-mass source. The v17 projected proxy

\[
q_g=\kappa_g{k_BT\over\mu m_pc^2}
\]

tests whether the *shape* of that independently measured random-stress field is
useful. It does not show that the minimal trace coupling can amplify it enough.
If the spatial pattern transfers, the action still needs a universal scale or
nonlinear invariant that explains the amplification.

## The four action-level routes that remain

### 1. Scalar stress-energy backreaction

Keep the conformal one-metric action and arrange the scalar solution so its own
positive stress-energy supplies the extra Weyl curvature. This is the smallest
field content, but the required scalar energy, screening, and stability must be
computed rather than assumed.

### 2. One universal disformal metric

Use

\[
\widetilde g_{\mu\nu}
=A^2(\sigma,X)g_{\mu\nu}
+B(\sigma,X)\nabla_\mu\sigma\nabla_\nu\sigma.
\]

The disformal term need not cancel from \(W\), so it can affect both light and
mass through the same metric. This is established Bekenstein/TeVeS prior art,
not novelty by itself. A Sigma version would need a distinctive invariant and
prediction while proving causal cones, tensor-wave speed, absence of ghosts,
and Solar-System screening.

### 3. Scalar-vector or aether completion

A unit timelike vector supplies a covariant local frame and directional
information that a scalar lacks. It can turn separated, crossing baryonic
flows into tensor structure capable of orienting shear. Einstein-aether, TeVeS,
and AeST are direct prior art. The vector must be dynamical; inserting an
analyst-defined galaxy or cluster coherence axis is not acceptable.

### 4. Nonlinear matter-stress coupling

An action can depend on invariants related to \(T\),
\(T_{\mu\nu}T^{\mu\nu}\), or projected spatial stress. Published
\(f(R,T)\) and energy-momentum-squared gravity already occupy this broad class.
Such a term can make pressure more important, but it often changes the matter
equations and stress conservation. A valid Sigma version must derive the
Landau-frame projector from dynamical fields, preserve universal free fall, and
show that the Hamiltonian remains bounded.

## Required action gate

No action advances merely because it can be adjusted to resemble the v17
source map. Before observational fitting it must provide all of the following:

1. one physical metric followed by stars, gas, laboratory bodies, and photons;
2. no object label, lens-only term, or per-system force parameter;
3. no more than five universal physical constants;
4. explicit Euler--Lagrange equations and a conserved total stress tensor;
5. positive kinetic and gradient terms and a well-posed principal part;
6. tensor-wave speed equal to light to \(10^{-15}\);
7. a derived weak-field pair \((\Psi,\Phi)\), not separately prescribed laws;
8. automatic suppression below the Solar-System limits; and
9. the same frozen equations used for galaxy dynamics and raw cluster lensing.

## Decision after the v17 source test

- If projected gas random stress fails the frozen transfer gates, do not invent
  a stress-coupled action to rescue it. Move to collisionless stress only after
  matched data exist, or to a materially different causal/nonlocal mechanism.
- If it passes, first test whether scalar stress-energy backreaction can supply
  the required Weyl curvature. If it cannot, the minimal serious completion is
  a universal disformal or dynamical-vector metric—not a photon multiplier.
- Any completion must be entered into the formula/prior-art registry before its
  first score.
