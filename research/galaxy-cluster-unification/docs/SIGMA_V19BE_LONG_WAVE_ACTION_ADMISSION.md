# Sigma V19BE long-wave action admission

## Decision

The proposal that gravity contains a mode whose wavelength is longer than a
star system is now a physical postulate of the active V19 source-state lane.
It supplies a plausible separation of scales, but it does not yet supply a
force law.

V19BE admits only a later **nonlinear, baryon-forced, source-state-sensitive,
one-metric action**. It rejects a free sinusoid, a hand-filtered source on the
right-hand side of Einstein's equation, a separately fitted wavelength for
each object, and a renamed version of the closed linear isotropic or Yukawa
tests. No action or Sigma constant is selected here.

## The idea in its cleanest form

Write the physical metric as a GR near field plus a possible additional mode,

\[
g_{\mu\nu}=g^{\rm GR}_{\mu\nu}+X_{\mu\nu}.
\]

A literal local wave representation would be

\[
X_{\mu\nu}=A_\Sigma e_{\mu\nu}
\cos(k_\alpha x^\alpha+\phi),
\qquad |\mathbf k|={2\pi\over\lambda_\Sigma}.
\]

If a laboratory or planetary system has size \(D\ll\lambda_\Sigma\), Taylor
expansion gives a constant piece, a nearly uniform first gradient and a tidal
piece. The constant piece is not a local gravitational observable. A uniform
acceleration can be removed at a point by choosing a freely falling frame.
The first invariant local signal is curvature,

\[
R[X]\sim \partial^2 X\sim
{A_\Sigma\over\lambda_\Sigma^2},
\]

so a relative effect measured over baseline \(D\) scales schematically as

\[
\Delta_{\rm tidal}\sim
A_\Sigma\left({D\over\lambda_\Sigma}\right)^2.
\]

This is the useful part of the hypothesis. A mode can be practically uniform
inside a star system and structured across a galaxy.

It is not enough to say that "gravity is a wave." GR already permits
gravitational waves of arbitrary wavelength, and a freely chosen long wave
does not explain why its phase, direction or amplitude should trace a galaxy.
The proposed mode must be generated by baryons and its homogeneous solution
must be fixed by one universal no-incoming or cosmological prescription.
Otherwise the arbitrary wave is simply an invisible halo encoded as initial
data.

For an illustrative correlation length \(L_\Sigma=6\) kpc and literal
wavelength \(\lambda_\Sigma=2\pi L_\Sigma=37.70\) kpc:

- the literal tidal factor across 100 AU is about \(1.7\times10^{-16}\);
- the small-baseline factor of the sourced low-pass control is about
  \(3.3\times10^{-15}\);
- at 10 kpc the same low-pass control reaches
  \(1-(1+r/L_\Sigma)e^{-r/L_\Sigma}\simeq0.496\); and
- the light-crossing time of the literal wavelength is about 123,000 years,

These values demonstrate scale separation only. The 6 kpc length is not a
selected constant, and this calculation does not establish Cassini, Mercury,
pulsar, radiation-loss or gravitational-wave propagation compliance.

## Why a filtered source cannot be added by hand

Einstein's tensor obeys the geometric identity

\[
\nabla_\mu G^{\mu\nu}=0.
\]

Suppose one writes

\[
G_{\mu\nu}={8\pi G\over c^4}
\left(T^b_{\mu\nu}+\alpha_\Sigma X_{\mu\nu}\right).
\]

Then the complete right-hand side must be covariantly conserved. Even when
the microscopic baryonic tensor satisfies \(\nabla_\mu T_b^{\mu\nu}=0\), a
curved-spacetime smoothing or retarded operation generally contains a
position-dependent kernel, parallel transport and boundary terms. It does not
follow that

\[
\nabla_\mu H_L[T_b]^{\mu\nu}=0.
\]

Therefore the appealing weak-field diagnostic

\[
(1-L_\Sigma^2\Box)X_{\mu\nu}=S_{\mu\nu}[T_b,j_b,\Pi_b]
\]

is not yet a valid metric equation. A later candidate must come from a
diffeomorphism-invariant action. Metric variation must produce both the added
field stress and any exchange terms, while the matter and added-field
equations enforce conservation. A traditional single-copy action with an
explicit retarded inverse can also return advanced pieces on variation; a
causal in-in or equivalent initial-value construction would have to be shown,
not asserted ([primary analysis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1601.03808)).

## Covariant baryonic source state

Let \(u^\mu\) be a covariantly specified baryonic matter frame and

\[
h_{\mu\nu}=g_{\mu\nu}+u_\mu u_\nu
\]

its spatial projector in units with \(c=1\). The baryonic stress-energy can be
decomposed as

\[
T_b^{\mu\nu}=\varepsilon u^\mu u^\nu
+2u^{(\mu}q^{\nu)}+p h^{\mu\nu}+\pi^{\mu\nu},
\]

where \(q^\mu\) is energy or species drift in the declared frame and
\(\pi^{\mu\nu}\) is trace-free stress. A collisionless merger and a thermal
gas can have the same density while having different \(q^\mu\), pressure,
stress, vorticity and causal assembly history. Those are covariant places
where the proposed long-wave response could acquire an orientation without a
"cluster" label.

The V19BA-V19BD products are only projected observational proxies for part of
this decomposition. They establish two useful facts in the spent pair:

- the velocity-second-moment centroid lies near 12% of one luminosity RMS
  radius from the luminosity centroid in both source ensembles; and
- the signed-current separation and orientation differ significantly between
  Bullet and Abell 2146.

The first fact suggests a transferable scale-free source feature. The second
rules out a fixed merger orientation. A universal operator would have to read
the actual source tensor. V19X is still required to add the gas state before
one combined source or action is chosen.

## Mechanism boundary

Three superficially similar routes are not equivalent:

| Route | Status | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| Free long sinusoid | prohibited | Its amplitude, phase and direction are arbitrary halo-equivalent initial data. |
| Linear isotropic low-pass/Yukawa response | closed control | The map-transfer version failed; the point-source version overlaps STVG/MOG and nonlocal-gravity prior art. |
| Nonlinear baryon-source-state interaction that leaves the GR quadratic propagator intact | admitted for later derivation | It can potentially retain the local GR pole while making galaxy/cluster geometry affect the nonlinear solution. It remains unproved. |

The ordinary identity-projector point-source reduction is the standard
Yukawa-like STVG/MOG form, not a Sigma discovery
([Moffat](https://arxiv.org/abs/gr-qc/0506021)). Causal nonlocal kernels that
imitate dark matter are also published prior art
([Mashhoon](https://arxiv.org/abs/1111.4702)). Entire-function metric form
factors are established as an action class
([Biswas et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1110.5249)). Our V3B audit additionally
showed that a standard positive spectral density cannot make the infrared
coupling exceed the locally calibrated ultraviolet coupling; its simplest
rational reversal has a negative massive residue.

Accordingly, changing only \(A_\Sigma\), \(L_\Sigma\), a Fourier exponent or a
projector name is not a new mechanism.

## Admission rules for the eventual action

A candidate can be selected after the gas source gate only if all of the
following are true:

1. All matter and photons couple to one physical metric.
2. A diffeomorphism-invariant action produces every metric and source-state
   equation and the complete stress-energy exchange.
3. The GR quadratic propagator and luminal tensor mode remain intact; the new
   response begins nonlinearly on the tested background.
4. One universal wavelength or correlation length is used for every Solar,
   galaxy and cluster calculation.
5. The source direction and activation are covariant functions of measured
   baryonic state, not object labels or fitted orientations.
6. The homogeneous state is fixed universally and cannot encode an arbitrary
   object halo.
7. The field's energy, constraints, kinetic signs, characteristics and causal
   initial-value problem are derived.
8. The complete theory uses at most five universal physical constants, no
   object-specific gravity constants and no lensing-only multiplier.
