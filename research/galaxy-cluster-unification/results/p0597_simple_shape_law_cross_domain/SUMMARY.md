# P0597 simple radial-shape law cross-domain replay

The post-hoc fixed law changes galaxy equal-weighted RMSE by +6.08% versus fixed RAR and improves 76/131 galaxies. All 5/5 fixed-formula folds improve; galaxy bootstrap probability is 98.5%. On three cluster holdouts it changes normalized morphology JSD by +19.54% versus local baryon light, improves 3/3 systems, and changes GLAFIC by +18.48%. The formula was chosen after P0596 disclosure and is therefore a candidate for future independent testing, not a validated discovery.
