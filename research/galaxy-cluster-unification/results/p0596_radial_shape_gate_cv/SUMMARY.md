# P0596 radial-shape gate cross-validation

The shape-gated family changed out-of-fold equal-galaxy RMSE by +3.26% versus fixed RAR and +1.82% versus the same diffuse family without a shape gate. It improved 73/131 galaxies and 5/5 held folds; bootstrap probability versus fixed RAR was 89.5%. The folds selected 5 distinct shape candidates.
