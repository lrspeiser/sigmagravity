# P0599 bounded potential amplitude

Whole-object cross-validation changed equal-cluster absolute RMSE from 0.509 to 0.120 dex (+76.5%) while galaxy equal-weighted RMSE was 10.883 km/s, a ratio of 1.015 to fixed RAR. It improved 20/20 clusters and 5/5 cluster folds. The strongest parameter impact was carrier with a median CLASH span of 0.161 dex. SPARC and CLASH potential support is separated by a factor 2.67, so intermediate BCG data are required before interpreting the threshold as universal physics.
