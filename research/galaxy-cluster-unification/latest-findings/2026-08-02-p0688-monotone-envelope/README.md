# P0688 monotone-envelope snapshot

Frozen before scores: 2026-08-02

## Bottom line

The smallest parameter-free repair to the topology-failing local-path
exponent also fails. It removes the central hollow and leaves galaxies at
`1.030x` fixed RAR, but scores `0.216 dex` across five spent clusters and
`0.271 dex` on the reliable three, closing 63.0% of the fixed-RAR gap. It does
not advance.

The repair nearly eliminates under-bending in MACS0329 and MACS0429, then
over-bends MACS1931, RXJ1347, and RXJ2129 by about `0.29-0.35 dex`. This shows
that another scalar exponent blend cannot distinguish the systems.

The P0684-P0688 local-path family is retired from further topology patches.
The next branch will change source placement while conserving total far-field
flux: route the positive extra QUMOND source onto the baryonic morphology and
place an equal compensating source on the already-defined potential-transition
shell. That directly tests gravity routing without a fitted amplitude or core
radius.
